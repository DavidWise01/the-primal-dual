# THE PRIMAL & THE DUAL · two problems, one value

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **DYAS**.

THE PRIMAL & THE DUAL — an OPTIMIZATION duality: a primal that maximizes output under limits has a dual that minimizes the value of those limits — different question, variable, shape — yet at the optimum they meet on the SAME number. ⚑ GREEN: for the shown LP (max 3x+2y / min 4y₁+6y₂) the primal optimum = 12 (vertex (4,0)) and the dual optimum = 12 (vertex (3,0)) — enumerated and node-verified, gap 0; WEAK duality (primal ≤ dual) holds for every feasible pair, and the live squeeze never lets the primal cross 12 or the dual fall below it. AMBER: 'resources vs prices / a problem and its shadow' is the standard economic reading (dual vars = shadow prices), an interpretation over the algebra; one concrete LP illustrates a general theorem. RED: strong duality here rests on LP feasibility — it can FAIL with a gap for some non-convex / infeasible / unbounded problems; a powerful duality with real hypotheses, not a universal law.
